Your visit

Practical information to help you plan your consultation and get the most from your time with Dr Goawalla.

Common
questions.

How do I arrange an appointment?

Call or email Nishad for The London Clinic, The Cromwell Hospital or Western Eye Hospital, on 07533 983835 or PA@londoneyesurgeon.uk. For LIPS Healthcare, call or email Nourhan on 020 3912 7263 or Goawalla.admin@lips.org.uk. Email is often the quickest way. You can also book directly with The London Clinic on 020 7935 4444 and The Cromwell Hospital on 020 7460 5700.

Do I need a referral?

Ask the secretary when arranging your appointment. Requirements can vary by clinic and by insurer. If you are using medical insurance, check whether a GP or optometrist referral and pre-authorisation are needed.

What should I bring?

Bring your glasses, a current medication list and any relevant referral letters, previous eye test results or scan reports. If you are using insurance, have your membership and authorisation details available.

Will I be able to drive afterwards?

Some eye examinations require drops that temporarily blur vision. Check with the clinic before your visit and plan transport if needed. Do not drive until your vision is safe and you have followed the advice given by your clinician.

Can I discuss premium lens implants?

Yes. Dr Goawalla can discuss lens options as part of a cataract assessment. Suitability depends on your eye health, visual priorities and the possible trade-offs of each lens.

What happens after a consultation?

Dr Goawalla will explain the assessment findings and discuss any recommended investigations, treatment or monitoring. You can ask questions about benefits, risks, alternatives, costs and next steps.

Where can I read patient reviews?

A selection is on the Dr Goawalla page, and all of them are on her Google and Trustpilot profiles. Reviews describe individual experiences and do not guarantee a particular treatment outcome.

Sudden change in vision? For sudden blurred vision, new flashes or floaters, or a dark curtain or shadow across your vision, seek urgent advice at an eye casualty department or through NHS 111. Do not wait for a routine appointment enquiry to be answered.

Privacy

This private website preview has no advertising trackers or analytics added by the practice. Referral letters uploaded on the For referrers page are sent by email to Dr Goawalla’s secretary and used only to arrange the patient’s care. The hosting provider may process technical access data to serve the website.

Phone and email links open your own calling or email app. External websites, including review services and maps, have their own privacy policies. For confidential clinical documents, ask the relevant clinic to confirm its secure transfer process.

The practice’s complete privacy notice will be added before the public website launches. For privacy enquiries, contact PA@londoneyesurgeon.uk.

Website information

This preview presents the proposed website and content for clinical review. General information cannot replace an individual examination, diagnosis or treatment plan.

Treatment suitability, benefits, alternatives, risks, recovery and fees are discussed individually. Outcomes vary and cannot be guaranteed. This website is not an emergency service.

The illustrations of vision on this website are simplified simulations, not predictions of any patient’s sight. Practice details are taken from the material supplied by Dr Goawalla. Photography includes colour- and clarity-enhanced versions of the supplied portraits.
