Clinics and appointments

Dr Goawalla sees patients at four clinics, all in central London and all very accessible. Which clinic suits you best usually depends on her availability on the day.

Two ways to book,
phone or email.

Call or email the secretary. Email is often the quickest way, and you can send it at any time of day.

You can also book directly with The London Clinic on 020 7935 4444 and The Cromwell Hospital on 020 7460 5700.

Privacy

This private website preview has no advertising trackers or analytics added by the practice. Referral letters uploaded on the For referrers page are sent by email to Dr Goawalla’s secretary and used only to arrange the patient’s care. The hosting provider may process technical access data to serve the website.

Phone and email links open your own calling or email app. External websites, including review services and maps, have their own privacy policies. For confidential clinical documents, ask the relevant clinic to confirm its secure transfer process.

The practice’s complete privacy notice will be added before the public website launches. For privacy enquiries, contact PA@londoneyesurgeon.uk.

Website information

This preview presents the proposed website and content for clinical review. General information cannot replace an individual examination, diagnosis or treatment plan.

Treatment suitability, benefits, alternatives, risks, recovery and fees are discussed individually. Outcomes vary and cannot be guaranteed. This website is not an emergency service.

The illustrations of vision on this website are simplified simulations, not predictions of any patient’s sight. Practice details are taken from the material supplied by Dr Goawalla. Photography includes colour- and clarity-enhanced versions of the supplied portraits.
